Why "Parmanu"? Truth Broken Down to Its Indivisible Atom.

In Sanskrit epistemology and classical Indian philosophy, Parmanu (परमाणु) signifies the ultimate indivisible building block of matter. For centuries, philosophers recognized that complex structures can only be understood by isolating their fundamental atoms.

In the modern digital era, journalism and media have devolved into monolithic, weaponized narratives. Sensationalist headlines conflate verified events with anonymous speculation, while viral algorithms reward shock value over evidentiary ground truth.

Parmanu was conceived as the antidote. We deconstruct every news dispatch into its atomic claims, independently cross-corroborate them against primary gazettes and verified records, and deliver the truth at the exact speed of information.
